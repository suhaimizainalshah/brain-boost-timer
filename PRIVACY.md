# Privacy policy: Brain Boost Time(r)

**Effective:** 24 November 2026
**Publisher:** Suhaimi bin Zainal Shah
**Contact:** suhaimizainalshah@gmail.com
**Covers:** the Chrome extension and the website version

## In short
Brain Boost Time(r) has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your own device. It does not read the websites you visit. Nothing is sent to the developer or anyone else unless you choose to share something yourself.

## What it keeps, and where
Everything below is stored only on your device: in Chrome’s local storage for the extension, or this browser’s local storage on the website. None of it is synced between devices.

| What | Why | How to remove it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Settings (flavour, boba, sound, chime, animation, straw angle, how the big screen opens) | So it looks and sounds the way you left it | Uninstall, or clear the site’s data |
| The running timer (its length, message and, for a lesson plan, its steps) | So a timer carries on if you close the big screen | Reset or Stop |
| Your timers: up to 8 recent timer lengths, and their messages if you allow it | One-click repeat of the timers you use | ✕ on each, Clear, or switch off “Remember my timers” or “…and their messages” |
| Lesson plans you save, and the one you’re editing (names of steps and their times) | To reuse your plans | Delete in My plans |
| Picker: the names you paste, or a number range, and who has been picked | To pick fairly, without repeats | “Put all back”, or “Clear the name list” |
| A count of finished timers per day, for up to 60 days | “Timers finished this week” | Uninstall, or clear the site’s data |
| Break reminders: minutes of active browsing since the last reminder | To remind you after an hour | Switch reminders off |

## What the extension can access
- **`idle`**: whether you are active, idle or away from your computer, so idle time isn’t counted towards a break reminder. It never sees what you are doing, and nothing is recorded except the count of active minutes.
- **`alarms`**: to wake at the end of a timer or lesson-plan step, and to count active minutes for break reminders.
- **`notifications`**: “Time’s up”, “Next step” and break reminder messages.
- **`storage`**: the items in the table above, on your device.
- **`contextMenus`**: the right-click menu on the toolbar icon (open the big screen, a 5-minute timer, the picker, break reminders and settings).

It has no access to websites, your browsing history, your tabs’ contents, your microphone or your camera. It uses no remote code.

## When something leaves your device: only if you choose
- **Sharing a lesson plan link** puts the plan’s name, step names and times into the link itself. Anyone you give the link to can see them.
- **“Share with other teachers”** opens a GitHub issue that you submit yourself. GitHub issues are **public**: only the plan’s name, step names and times are included, and you are asked not to add students’ names or other personal details. GitHub’s own privacy statement applies to your GitHub account.
- **“Recommend a flavour or request a feature”** opens Gmail, Outlook or your email app with a message you write and send yourself, to the developer’s address. It adds the version and the flavour in use, nothing else.
- **Downloading or opening a plan file** happens only on your device.

## The website version
The website stores the same items in this browser’s local storage and sets no cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub receives the usual technical details of any web visit (such as your IP address), under GitHub’s privacy statement; the developer receives none of this. If you allow notifications, the browser shows them; nothing is sent. The shared plan library is read from the same website.

## Students and children
Brain Boost Time(r) is built for classrooms. It collects no information from anyone, including students and children. Names typed into the picker stay on the teacher’s device. We suggest using first names or register numbers, and **Hide the list** on a projected screen. On shared computers, consider switching off “…and their messages” under Remember my timers.

## Deleting everything
Uninstall the extension, or clear the website’s data in your browser’s settings. Both remove everything listed above.
