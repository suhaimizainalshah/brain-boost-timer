# Chrome Web Store listing: Brain Boost Time(r)

## Name (must match manifest.json)
Brain Boost Time(r): Break Reminder, Class Timer and Name Picker

## Summary (132 characters max)
Hourly break reminders, a melting bubble tea class timer (type bbt 5m in the address bar) and a boba name picker.

## Description
Brain Boost Time(r) helps classrooms and anyone at a screen take healthy breaks.

BREAK REMINDERS
• After an hour of active browsing, a friendly reminder suggests a Brain Boost Time break.
• Click the toolbar icon to switch reminders off: the icon turns grey.

A TIMER YOU START FROM THE ADDRESS BAR
• Type bbt, a space, then a time: bbt 5m Stretch and drink water, bbt 90s, bbt 2:30 Group work.
• The big screen shows a bubble tea whose ice melts and whose tea lightens as time runs out.

LESSON PLANS
• Build a run of timers (Do now, Group work, Pack up) that moves on by itself, and share plans with other teachers.

FULL SCREEN FOR PROJECTORS
• Giant numbers beside the melting bubble tea, readable from the back of the room.

YOUR TIMERS
• Your recent and most-used timers, one click away, kept only on your device.

A BOBA NAME PICKER
• Paste a class list; click the straw and a boba carries a name up and out. Names stay hidden until picked.

MAKE IT YOURS
• Fourteen flavours, from taro and matcha to grape and mango, with popping boba to match.

PRIVATE
• No accounts, no tracking, no network requests. Everything stays in your browser.

Brain Boost Time began as a classroom tool that received a Gold Innergy Award 2022 from the Ministry of Education, Singapore.

## Single purpose
Helping people take timely breaks from screen time: reminders, a break timer and a picker for classroom breaks.

## Privacy practices (paste into the Developer Dashboard)

### Single purpose
Brain Boost Time(r) helps teachers and learners take regular, timed brain breaks: a bubble tea class timer (including lesson plans of timed steps), a name and number picker for the class, and gentle reminders to take a break after an hour of active browsing.

### alarms justification
Wakes the extension at the end of a timer or a lesson-plan step, even when the big screen is closed, to show “Time’s up” or move to the next step. A repeating alarm also counts minutes of active browsing for the hourly break reminder, and updates the countdown shown on the toolbar icon.

### notifications justification
Shows the user a system notification when their timer ends, when a lesson plan moves to its next step, and when it is time for a break, so they see it even if Chrome is in the background. Notifications contain only the timer’s message or the step’s name.

### storage justification
Keeps the user’s settings, the running timer, their recent timers, saved lesson plans, the picker’s names or number range, and a private count of finished timers, on their own device only. Nothing is synced or sent anywhere.

### idle justification
Checks only whether the user is active, idle or locked, so that time away from the computer is not counted towards the hourly break reminder. It does not see or record what the user is doing; only a count of active minutes is kept, on the device.

### contextMenus justification
Adds options to the right-click menu on the toolbar icon: open the big screen, start a 5-minute timer, open the picker, switch break reminders on or off, and change settings (sound, animation, remembering timers, and whether the big screen opens in a window or a tab). The menu acts only within the extension and reads no page content.
