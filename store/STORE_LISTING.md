# Chrome Web Store listing: Brain Boost Time(r)

## Name (must match manifest.json)
Brain Boost Time(r): Break Reminder, Class Timer and Name Picker

## Summary (132 characters max)
Hourly break reminders, a melting bubble tea class timer (type bbt 5m in the address bar) and a boba name picker.

## Description
Brain Boost Time(r) helps classrooms and anyone at a screen take healthy breaks.

BREAK REMINDERS
• After an hour of active browsing, a friendly reminder suggests a Brain Boost Time break.
• Click the toolbar icon to switch reminders off: the icon turns grey.

A TIMER YOU START FROM THE ADDRESS BAR
• Type bbt, a space, then a time: bbt 5m Stretch and drink water, bbt 90s, bbt 2:30 Group work.
• The big screen shows a bubble tea whose ice melts and whose tea lightens as time runs out.

LESSON PLANS
• Build a run of timers (Do now, Group work, Pack up) that moves on by itself, and share plans with other teachers.

FULL SCREEN FOR PROJECTORS
• Giant numbers beside the melting bubble tea, readable from the back of the room.

YOUR TIMERS
• Your recent and most-used timers, one click away, kept only on your device.

A BOBA NAME PICKER
• Paste a class list; click the straw and a boba carries a name up and out. Names stay hidden until picked.

MAKE IT YOURS
• Fourteen flavours, from taro and matcha to grape and mango, with popping boba to match.

PRIVATE
• No accounts, no tracking, no network requests. Everything stays in your browser.

Brain Boost Time began as a classroom tool that received a Gold Innergy Award 2022 from the Ministry of Education, Singapore.

## Single purpose
Helping people take timely breaks from screen time: reminders, a break timer and a picker for classroom breaks.

## Permission justifications
• alarms: break reminders and timers that keep time when the extension is asleep.
• notifications: tells you when a timer ends.
• storage: keeps your settings and names on this device.
• idle: so idle time doesn’t count towards a break reminder.
• contextMenus: the toolbar icon’s right-click menu.

## Data usage
No user data is collected or transmitted.
